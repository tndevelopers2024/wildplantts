What is 3D Laser Scanning?

3D Laser Scanning is a Reality Capture technology that uses Terrestrial LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) to measure the physical environment with millimetre-level accuracy.

A laser scanner rotates 360 degrees while emitting thousands of laser pulses every second. These pulses measure the distance to surrounding objects and generate a dense 3D Point Cloud, accurately representing the geometry of buildings, machinery, piping, structural steel, and infrastructure.
