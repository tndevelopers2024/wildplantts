What is Scan to BIM?

Scan to BIM is the process of converting 3D Reality Capture data into intelligent Building Information Models (BIM).

Instead of manually measuring existing buildings, Reality Capture technologies generate highly accurate point clouds that represent every visible element of a structure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Oil & Gas Plant 3D Scanning?

A LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) Laser Scanner is a precision instrument that uses laser light to measure distances. It sends out thousands of laser pulses per second, measures how long they take to bounce back, and uses that data to create highly accurate 3D representations of physical environments.

Why use LiDAR in refineries?

LiDAR provides high-precision as-built documentation safely and quickly without disrupting operations.

Can you scan operating oil & gas facilities?

Yes, we specialize in scanning operating facilities safely and efficiently without halting your continuous processes.

Can production continue during scanning?

Absolutely, non-contact laser scanning allows capture from safe distances without interfering with your production.

Can you create AVEVA E3D models?

Yes, our teams are proficient in producing accurate AVEVA E3D deliverables integrated seamlessly with your systems.
