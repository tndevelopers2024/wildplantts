Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Tank 3D Scanning?

A LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) Laser Scanner is a precision instrument that uses laser light to measure distances. It sends out thousands of laser pulses per second, measures how long they take to bounce back, and uses that data to create highly accurate 3D representations of physical environments.

2. How accurate is LiDAR scanning?

LiDAR scanning provides millimeter-level accuracy, ensuring that all dimensions, clearances, and structural details are captured precisely for reliable engineering analysis and retrofitting design.

3. Can you scan large tanks?

Yes, our high-precision terrestrial laser scanners have extensive range capabilities allowing them to capture massive storage tanks effortlessly, ensuring complete coverage with high point density.

4. Why hire a professional team?

A professional team brings domain expertise, certified scanning equipment, and rigorous QA/QC processes. This ensures safety compliance on site and guarantees that the point cloud data provided is accurately georeferenced and ready for integration.

5. Can you create CAD models?

Absolutely. We specialize in post-processing precision point cloud data into intelligent 2D/3D CAD and BIM models tailored explicitly to your project specifications and engineering constraints.
