What is Building Scanning?

Building Scanning is the process of capturing the complete geometry of a building using Reality Capture technologies.

Millions of laser measurements are collected to generate a highly detailed 3D Point Cloud, accurately representing:

  • Building Structure
  • Mechanical Systems
  • Interior Spaces
  • Structural Elements
  • Exterior Facades
  • Roofs
  • Architectural Features
  • Utilities

This digital dataset becomes the foundation for engineering design, renovation, BIM modelling, facility management, and Digital Twin development.

Why Building Scanning?

Traditional building documentation often suffers from:

  • Missing drawings
  • Outdated floor plans
  • Unrecorded renovations
  • Inaccurate dimensions
  • Manual measurement errors
  • Unknown structural changes

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Why should I scan my building before renovation?

    Building Scanning provides accurate existing conditions, reducing design errors, construction conflicts, and unexpected costs during renovation.

  • Can occupied buildings be scanned?

    Yes. Most commercial, residential, educational, and healthcare facilities can be scanned while remaining operational, subject to access and safety requirements.

  • Can you create BIM models from the scan?

    Yes. We provide Revit models, IFC models, LOD BIM models, CAD drawings, and As-Built documentation.

  • Do you scan heritage buildings?

    Yes. We specialize in non-contact Reality Capture for heritage structures, monuments, museums, and historically significant buildings.

  • Can the scan data be used for facility management?

    Absolutely. Building scan data forms the foundation for Asset Registers, BIM to FM workflows, Digital Twins, and long-term building management.
