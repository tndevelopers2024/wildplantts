Overview

Leica BLK360: Precision 3D Scanning for Indian Interiors and Architectural Detailing

The Leica BLK360 is a lightweight, high-speed 3D laser scanner designed for compact and mid-sized Indian project sites. It’s ideal for:

  • Capturing floor plans and interior layouts in metro cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Chennai

  • Documenting heritage structures for digital archiving and restoration projects in places like Jaipur, Varanasi, and Hampi

  • Generating quick, accurate models for renovation, retrofitting, or smart city projects

With a 20-second scan cycle, it rapidly captures 3D data and spherical imagery. Integrated VIS (Visual Inertial System) technology auto-aligns scans on-site, cutting down post-processing time and ensuring clean, complete datasets before leaving the location.

Why it fits the Indian market:

  • Captures precise spatial data at 360,000 points per second, ideal for fast-paced project environments

  • Ultra-lightweight and portable—easy to transport across job sites, even in congested urban settings

  • Built-in panoramic camera delivers vivid HDR imagery for clear visual documentation

  • Effective for short-range scanning up to 60 meters—perfect for indoor, facility, and site-level work

Key Features

BLK360: Power-Packed Features for Fast, Accurate 3D Reality Capture

Engineered for speed, precision, and simplicity, the Leica BLK360 brings cutting-edge scanning technology to your fingertips—wherever the job takes you.

True-to-Life HDR Imaging

Equipped with four 13 MP cameras and advanced 5-bracket HDR, the BLK360 produces richly detailed, color-accurate imagery. From low-light environments to sunlit interiors, you get high-contrast photospheres and colorized point clouds that enhance visual clarity.

Effortless Scan Alignment with VIS Technology

Leica’s Visual Inertial System (VIS) automatically aligns scans in the field, streamlining workflows and minimizing manual registration back in the office—saving you time and effort.

Lightning-Fast Scan Speed

Capture up to 680,000 points per second and complete full scans with imagery in just 20 seconds. Choose from multiple scan profiles to balance speed and detail based on your project needs.

Quick 2D Layouts on Site

With Cyclone FIELD 360, you can instantly generate 2D floor plans and layout drawings from your 3D scan data—right on-site, even before leaving the project location.

Fast & Reliable Data Transfer

Thanks to its USB-C connection, large scan files are transferred quickly and securely without relying solely on Wi-Fi, making data management more efficient.

Seamless Cloud Collaboration

The BLK360 integrates with Reality Cloud Studio by Hexagon, allowing you to upload, view, and collaborate on scan data in the cloud—anytime, from anywhere.

Smart Mobile Control with Cyclone FIELD 360

Easily operate the scanner using the Cyclone FIELD 360 mobile app (available for iOS and Android). Monitor scans in real time, manage projects on the go, and pre-register data on-site. (Requires a valid Cyclone REGISTER or REGISTER 360 [BLK Edition] license.)

Solution 360

Complete 360° Laser Imaging Solution

The Leica BLK360 system is built to provide a complete, streamlined solution for fast, accurate 3D reality capture—from data collection to processing. The package includes:

Leica BLK360

A highly portable, compact laser scanner designed for speed and ease of use, delivering detailed point clouds and high-dynamic-range (HDR) images with exceptional mobility.

Leica Cyclone FIELD 360

A mobile application that lets users operate the scanner remotely and enables real-time pre-registration of scans using Visual Inertial System (VIS) technology.

Leica Cyclone REGISTER 360 (BLK Edition)

Desktop software for organizing and aligning multiple scans into a unified point cloud dataset, ideal for post-processing after fieldwork.

BLK Live

A free companion app that provides instant access to scanned data, allowing users to manage the scanner and review scans on-site for immediate quality checks and feedback.
