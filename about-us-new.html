WHO WE ARE

Engineering Reality. Enabling Tomorrow.

WildplantTS is the dedicated Reality Capture and Digital Engineering division of the Precise3DM Group, focused exclusively on helping organizations capture, understand, and digitally transform the built environment.

We specialize in advanced Reality Capture technologies including Terrestrial LiDAR, Mobile SLAM, Drone LiDAR, Drone Photogrammetry, 360° Reality Capture, GIS, BIM, Digital Twins, and Spatial Computing, creating accurate digital representations of real-world assets for engineering, construction, infrastructure, utilities, and industrial applications.

Beyond providing professional Reality Capture and Digital Engineering services, WildplantTS collaborates with leading global hardware and software technology partners to bring the latest Reality Capture innovations to the market. Through strategic partnerships with world-renowned OEMs such as EPIC LiDAR, Hexagon Leica Geosystems, and other leading technology providers, we deliver cutting-edge Reality Capture solutions backed by expert consulting, implementation, training, workflow development, and long-term technical support.

Our mission extends beyond engineering services. We help organisations establish in-house Reality Capture capabilities through strategic CAPEX investments by supplying the right hardware, software, implementation expertise, workflow consulting, and user training. From technology selection to deployment and operational support, we enable customers to build sustainable digital engineering ecosystems.

Whether you require a one-time scanning project, an enterprise-scale Digital Twin, or a long-term technology implementation partner, WildplantTS delivers complete end-to-end solutions—from technology selection and data capture to engineering deliverables and lifecycle asset management.
