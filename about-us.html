About Us

WildplantTS is a technology-driven company dedicated to transforming terrestrial objects using advanced digital solutions. We specialise in delivering innovative services for buildings, infrastructure, industrial facilities, transportation systems, and agriculture. By leveraging LiDAR, drone-based mapping, and BIM technologies, we help businesses visualise, manage, and optimise their physical assets with exceptional accuracy and efficiency.

We Operate With Two Core Business Models

  • 1. Equipment Sales – Helping Companies Invest In The Right LiDAR 3D Scanners And Software Solutions.
  • 2. LiDAR-Based Services – Providing End-To-End Services, Including In-House Scan-To-BIM Modeling And Scan-To-Factory CAD Capabilities.

What We Do

WildplantTs offers advanced terrestrial LiDAR 3D laser-scanning solutions and Facility reality capture by 3D scanning and converting the data into a 3D model for industries across India. As an authorized partner for Hexagon Geospatial, we support clients in adopting advanced technology with complete pre- and post-sales support.

We also specialize in 3D laser scanning for factories (digital twins), buildings, infrastructure, heritage sites, movie sets, terrains, and shipbuilding.

Our expertise also includes 3D modeling and scan-to-CAD/BIM services, such as Scan to Factory CAD, Plant 3D, and BIM conversion.

From scanner sales to end-to-end project execution, we deliver accurate, efficient, and reliable digital solutions.
