What is Factory Scanning?

Factory Scanning is the process of digitally capturing an entire manufacturing facility using advanced Reality Capture technologies.
Millions of laser measurements are collected to create an accurate 3D Point Cloud representing every visible component within the factory.

This digital dataset can then be converted into:

Factory Layouts

CAD Drawings

BIM Models

Plant3D Models

Asset Registers

Digital Twins

Engineering Documentation

The result is a reliable digital foundation for future engineering projects and operational improvements.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why should I scan my factory?
Factory Scanning creates an accurate digital record that supports expansion, maintenance, automation, retrofit engineering, and Digital Twin implementation while reducing costly design errors.
2. Can the factory remain operational during scanning?
Yes. In many cases, scanning can be performed with minimal interruption to production, following your site's safety and operational requirements.
3. Can you create BIM and CAD models from the scan?
Yes. We provide Scan to CAD, Scan to BIM, Plant3D models, Revit models, IFC models, and Digital Twin deliverables.
4. How often should a factory be rescanned?
Many organizations perform a baseline scan and then update it after major equipment installations, plant expansions, or significant layout changes.
5. Can you integrate factory data into Facility Management systems?
Yes. Our deliverables can support Asset Registers, QR-based asset management, BIM to FM workflows, and Digital Twin platforms.
