Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why use LiDAR in cement plants?
A LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) Laser Scanner is a precision instrument that uses laser light to measure distances. It sends out thousands of laser pulses per second, measures how long they take to bounce back, and uses that data to create highly accurate 3D representations of physical environments.
2. Can you scan an operating cement plant?
Yes, laser scanning is completely non-contact and safe. We can perform rapid captures from safe ground distances without interfering with your ongoing production.
3. Can production continue during scanning?
Absolutely. Our scanning processes ensure zero downtime for your facility. We map existing structures dynamically while operations run normally.
4. How accurate is terrestrial LiDAR?
Terrestrial LiDAR systems capture positional geometry with millimeter-grade accuracy, vastly outperforming traditional string or tape survey methods.
5. Can you model complex steel structures?
Yes, dense point clouds allow our engineers to reverse-engineer and precisely model complex multi-level structural steel networks perfectly.
6. Can you create Plant 3D models?
Our CAD experts natively support integration into AutoCAD Plant 3D, providing intelligent pipelines and equipment tracking metrics.
7. Can you create intelligent CAD?
Yes, models can be authored with embedded meta-data, converting simple geometries into fully intelligent BIM architectural components.
8. Can you scan rotary kilns?
We reliably scan kiln shells and associated rotational assemblies to monitor deviations, wear, and structural deflections safely.
9. Can you scan AQC structures?
Yes, we maneuver specialized riggers to acquire full scans of Air Quenching Cooler complexes, generating immediate as-built evaluations.
10. Can you scan Preheater towers?
Yes, even multi-tier 100-meter preheater towers can be completely scanned and spatially locked to map ducting clearances.
11. Can you scan conveyors?
Scanning long-span material handling conveyors is highly efficient, allowing for precise structural alignment calculations.
12. Can you perform clash detection?
By overlaying proposed 3D design models into the scanned site point-cloud, we easily pre-empt spatial clashes before construction.
13. Can you create digital twins?
Photorealistic colored point clouds serve as the foundational backbone for establishing live, interactive facility Digital Twins.
14. Can scans be used for retrofit projects?
Scan-to-CAD is explicitly engineered to guarantee 100% first-time fitment when mapping out mechanical plant retrofits.
15. Do you travel across India?
Our qualified engineering deployment crews mobilize Pan-India to efficiently conduct on-site industrial mapping tasks.
16. How long does scanning take?
Modern terrestrial hardware analyzes up to 2 million points per second, meaning total site downtime drops from months down to merely days.
17. Can you scan shutdown projects?
Yes, we excel during critical limited-window turnaround plant shutdown schedules, capturing environments at peak speed 24/7.
18. Can you support EPC contractors?
We provide full-spectrum digital surveys feeding right into the workflow requirements of massive national EPC project firms.
19. Can you model pipelines?
We model existing ducting, pipe geometries, and flanges utilizing intelligent algorithms for incredibly precise fabrication tie-ins.
20. Can you provide BIM models?
Yes, from LOD 100 to extreme LOD 500 integrations, we output highly refined BIM topologies native to all major platforms.
