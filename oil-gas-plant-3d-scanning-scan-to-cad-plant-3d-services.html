Frequently Asked Questions

What is Oil & Gas Plant 3D Scanning?
A LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) Laser Scanner is a precision instrument that uses laser light to measure distances. It sends out thousands of laser pulses per second, measures how long they take to bounce back, and uses that data to create highly accurate 3D representations of physical environments.
Why use LiDAR in refineries?
LiDAR provides millimeters-accurate, highly reliable point cloud data that eliminates manual surveying errors, massively reduces project turnaround times, and minimizes safety risks for personnel.
Can you scan operating oil & gas facilities?
Yes, our equipment and workflows are fully certified to capture data across highly complex operational facilities without disrupting active production cycles.
Can production continue during scanning?
Absolutely. 3D Laser Scanning is a fully non-contact and non-destructive methodology. Production naturally continues entirely unimpeded.
Do you use Leica RTC360?
Yes, we rely heavily on the Leica RTC360 for its extreme speed and sub-millimeter precision when mapping densely packed engineering grids.
Do you use FARO Focus?
Yes, we utilize the FARO Focus series for highly flexible, HDR-colored, long-range terrestrial scanning implementations.
Can you create Autodesk Plant 3D models?
Yes, our dedicated engineering suite routinely outputs full-feature intelligent 3D models populated completely within Autodesk Plant 3D.
Can you create AVEVA E3D models?
Yes, we maintain an expert team of modelers specialized in developing intricate plant environments securely within AVEVA E3D limits.
Can you model pipe racks?
Certainly, our reality capture team captures the precise spatial topography of pipe racks, allowing layout validation down to the structural weld profiles.
Can you model equipment?
Yes, everything from standard rotary pumps and compressors directly up to large-scale distillation columns is successfully mapped and geometrically translated.
Can you create intelligent piping models?
Yes, our digital twin data acts as the absolute geometrical foundation used to encode full ISO-compliant intelligent piping attributes.
Can you scan storage tanks?
Yes, we thoroughly scan both the interior layouts and full exterior shells of massive storage tanks for structural analysis and tank calibration computations.
Can you support shutdown projects?
Yes, our rapid mobilization capacity is fundamentally aligned with the extreme time-sensitivity required across shutdown maintenance periods.
Can you scan offshore facilities?
Yes, our technicians deploy safely across FPSOs, jack-up rigs, and offshore extraction facilities for extensive digital twinning.
Can you create digital twins?
Absolutely. High-fidelity reality capture serves as the geometric baseline required to structure powerful operational data into a true digital twin platform.
How accurate is LiDAR?
Terrestrial LiDAR scanners reliably process data clusters falling within a ±1-2mm tolerance envelope across standard working distances.
Do you provide Pan India services?
Yes, our teams operate locally out of strategic nodes permitting rapid cross-country mobilization anywhere within Pan India regions.
Can you support EPC contractors?
Yes, we partner continually with massive global EPC firms streamlining brownfield execution via flawless integration of absolute reality modeling.
How long does a refinery scan take?
This is intensely dependent on facility footprint size and pipe density complexity. Scanning periods easily range from 2 days up to several weeks for colossal complexes.
What file formats do you deliver?
We directly yield standardized mesh constraints alongside raw clouds supporting direct ingress for standard engineering pathways (E57, RCS/RCP, STEP, IGES, DWG, DXF, RVT, NWD).
