The PRECISE S7 is a professional handheld mobile LiDAR scanner designed for fast, accurate 3D reality capture across buildings, infrastructure, industrial facilities, construction sites, terrain and complex environments.

Combining MLF-SLAM, RTK, PPK, GNSS, LiDAR, IMU and panoramic imaging, the S7 allows surveyors, engineers and reality-capture professionals to collect detailed 3D spatial data while walking through the project.

With up to 200,000 points per second, a maximum detection range of 70 m, and absolute accuracy of <3 cm in post-processing, the S7 is built for professional mobile mapping applications.
