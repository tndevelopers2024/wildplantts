What is Mobile SLAM?

Mobile SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) is an advanced Reality Capture technology that combines:

LiDAR Sensors IMU Cameras SLAM Algorithms Artificial Intelligence

to continuously determine the scanner's position while simultaneously mapping the surrounding environment.

Unlike traditional terrestrial laser scanners that require multiple static setups, Mobile SLAM allows operators to simply walk through a facility while the system captures the environment continuously.

The result is a highly accurate 3D point cloud generated in a fraction of the time.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. When should Mobile SLAM be used instead of Terrestrial LiDAR?

Mobile SLAM is ideal when rapid capture of large indoor environments is the priority. For projects requiring the highest engineering accuracy, terrestrial LiDAR may be more appropriate, or both technologies can be combined.

2. Can Mobile SLAM data be converted into BIM?

Yes. In many cases, scanning can be performed with minimal interruption to production, following your site's safety and operational requirements.

3. Can you create BIM and CAD models from the scan?

Yes. We convert Mobile SLAM point clouds into Revit models, IFC models, CAD drawings, Facility Models, and Digital Twins.

4. Does Mobile SLAM work without GPS?

Yes. SLAM technology is specifically designed to operate indoors where GPS signals are unavailable.

5. Can operational facilities be scanned?

Yes. Most factories, hospitals, warehouses, airports, and commercial buildings can be scanned while remaining operational, subject to safety and access requirements.

6. Is Mobile SLAM suitable for Digital Twin projects?

Absolutely. Mobile SLAM provides a fast and efficient way to capture the spatial data required for Building, Factory, Campus, and Facility Digital Twins.
