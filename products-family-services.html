Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which product is suitable for my project?
Our specialists recommend the most suitable technology based on your site size, accuracy requirements, deliverables and project objectives.
2. Do I need both hardware and software?
Not always. You can purchase a complete solution, individual products, or use our engineering services if you prefer an OPEX model.
3. Can your products integrate with BIM and GIS platforms?
Yes. Our workflows support BIM, CAD, GIS, Digital Twin, Facility Management and OpenBIM standards.
4. Can I start with scanning services and later purchase equipment?
Absolutely. Many customers begin with our engineering services before investing in their own Reality Capture systems.
5. Do you provide installation and training?
Yes. We provide installation, workflow implementation, user training and long-term technical support.
