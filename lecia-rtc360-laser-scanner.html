Overview

Experience efficient 3D reality capture with the Leica RTC360 LT—designed to make high-performance laser scanning more accessible and economical to Indian customers.

  • Fast, Vivid 3D Scanning in Minutes – With the ability to capture up to 1 million points per second and equipped with an advanced HDR imaging system, this agile solution delivers vivid, colourised 3D point clouds in less than two minutes.

  • Portable Design for On-the-Go Professionals – Built for mobility and ease of use, the RTC360 LT features a lightweight, backpack-friendly design and a collapsible tripod, making it ideal for on-the-go professionals. Its one-button operation ensures quick setup and minimal training—perfect for fast-paced fieldwork.

  • Precision Scanning with Real-Time Verification – Achieve high-quality, low-noise data that brings every detail to life. When paired with Cyclone FIELD 360, users benefit from semi-automated, targetless registration and can verify scan quality in real time—saving hours of manual effort and increasing productivity from site to office.

From construction zones to industrial facilities and complex infrastructure projects, the RTC360 is built to perform reliably—even in the challenging environments often encountered across India.
