Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is pharmaceutical plant 3D scanning?
A LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) Laser Scanner is a precision instrument that uses laser light to measure distances. It sends out thousands of laser pulses per second, measures how long they take to bounce back, and uses that data to create highly accurate 3D representations of physical environments.
2. What is Scan-to-BIM?
Scan-to-BIM is the process of using 3D laser scanning to capture real-world conditions of a building or facility and converting that rich data into a highly accurate 3D Building Information Model (BIM).
3. Can you scan operating pharmaceutical facilities?
Yes, our LiDAR scanning equipment relies on non-invasive laser technology which allows us to safely and efficiently scan fully operational facilities without disrupting your day-to-day manufacturing processes.
4. How accurate is Leica RTC360 scanning?
The Leica RTC360 laser scanner is an industrial-grade precision instrument that delivers survey-grade millimeter accuracy (typically up to 1-2mm depending on environmental conditions).
5. What BIM LOD levels do you provide?
We provide BIM models ranging from Level of Development (LOD) 100 which represents basic conceptual forms, all the way up to highly-detailed LOD 400 and LOD 500 representing specialized fabrication and as-built geometry depending on project scope.
6. Can you model cleanrooms?
Absolutely. Cleanrooms require strict contamination control, and our non-contact LiDAR devices permit rigorous and rapid spatial capturing without risking sterilization protocols or compromising hygienic standards.
7. Can you scan HVAC systems?
Yes, our advanced 3D scanning captures complex MEP installations, piping routes, and intricate HVAC systems seamlessly, granting you an accurate digital twin of extremely congested overhead environments.
8. Can you scan utility tunnels?
Yes, we regularly scan complex and confined spaces including underground utility tunnels and shafts layout to securely map pipelines, electrical trays, and structural parameters for maintenance and safety audits.
9. Can you create Revit BIM models?
Yes, our specialized engineering team directly processes raw point cloud data into fully intelligent Autodesk Revit BIM models tailored to your structural, architectural, and MEP requirements.
10. Can you perform clash detection?
We heavily utilize Navisworks and Revit clash detection logic to digitally cross-reference existing structures against new proposed retrofit designs, completely eliminating expensive field clashes easily.
11. Can you support brownfield expansion?
Yes, our Scan-to-BIM workflow is fundamentally engineered to support brownfield expansions by precisely mapping out existing constraints, seamlessly blending as-built data with your new architectural propositions.
12. Can you deliver IFC models?
Yes, alongside native formats such as .rvt, .dwg, and .nwd, we deliver open-standard Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) models allowing you complete software independence and massive structural flexibility.
13. How long does Scan-to-BIM take?
The duration strongly depends on the square footage of the facility. However, our high-speed LiDAR methodology often slashes traditional manual survey times by 70%, with models commonly delivered within just a few weeks.
14. Do you travel across India?
Yes, we provide comprehensive pan-India service coverage. Our rapid mobilization teams carry highly portable LiDAR equipment fully prepared to tackle facilities anywhere across the country on short notice.
15. How much does pharmaceutical plant 3D scanning cost?
Costs vary based on the specific total area of the facility, complexity of the piping and assets, and the requested Level of Detail (LOD) for the final BIM deliverable. Please contact us for a rapid direct quotation!
