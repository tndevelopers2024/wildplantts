ABOUT

WildplantTS is a technology-driven company dedicated to transforming terrestrial objects using advanced digital solutions. We specialise in delivering innovative services for buildings, infrastructure, industrial facilities, transportation systems, and agriculture. By leveraging LiDAR, drone-based mapping, and BIM technologies, we help businesses visualise, manage, and optimise their physical assets with exceptional accuracy and efficiency.

We operate with two core business models

  • 1. Equipment Sales – Helping companies invest in the right LiDAR 3D scanners and software solutions.

  • 2. LiDAR-Based Services – Providing end-to-end services, including in-house Scan-to-BIM modeling and Scan-to-Factory CAD capabilities.
