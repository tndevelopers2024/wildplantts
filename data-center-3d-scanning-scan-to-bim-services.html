Frequently Asked Questions

What is Oil & Gas Plant 3D Scanning?
A LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) Laser Scanner is a precision instrument that uses laser light to measure distances. It sends out thousands of laser pulses per second, measures how long they take to bounce back, and uses that data to create highly accurate 3D representations of physical environments.
Why use LiDAR in refineries?
LiDAR provides rapid, highly accurate, and non-contact 3D measurements of complex refinery environments. This ensures enhanced safety, minimizes downtime, and delivers precise data for retrofitting, maintenance, and facility management.
Can you scan operating oil & gas facilities?
Yes, our advanced scanning equipment is designed to operate safely within active oil and gas facilities, capturing detailed spatial data without disrupting ongoing operations.
Can production continue during scanning?
Absolutely. 3D laser scanning is a non-intrusive process, allowing your plant's production to continue seamlessly while we capture the necessary data.
Do you use Leica RTC360?
Yes, we utilize the Leica RTC360 laser scanner to ensure high-speed, accurate, and comprehensive 3D reality capture for large-scale industrial projects.
Do you use FARO Focus?
Yes, we use FARO Focus scanners to deliver reliable and highly precise measurements, especially in complex and space-constrained industrial environments.
Can you create Autodesk Plant 3D models?
Yes, we have extensive experience in transforming raw point cloud data into intelligent and highly accurate Autodesk Plant 3D models tailored to your engineering needs.
Can you create AVEVA E3D models?
Yes, our team is proficient in creating detailed and data-rich AVEVA E3D models to support advanced engineering and design workflows in the oil and gas sector.
Can you model pipe racks?
Yes, we can accurately model complex pipe racks, capturing all structural components, pipelines, and fittings with millimeter-level precision.
Can you model equipment?
Yes, we create highly detailed 3D models of all types of plant equipment, including vessels, pumps, compressors, and custom machinery, ensuring accurate integration into your overall facility model.
