OVERVIEW

INFRASTRUCTURE 3D SCANNING

Built to last. Civil infrastructure is crucial to the continuity of our daily lives and our economies. From highways, airports, and railways to ports, bridges, and tunnels, civil projects need to be built, managed, and renovated with the highest standards in order to stay safe and connected. With 3D laser scanning solutions, surveyors can add significant value throughout the lifecycle of civil infrastructure as they collect complex data very quickly in all forms of terrain while still maintaining a high level of accuracy. With the rapid technological advances in laser scanning, Wildplant terrestrial solutions provide advanced Factory 3D laser scanning to utilize the latest technology available.
