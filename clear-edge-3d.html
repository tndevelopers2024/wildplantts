Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClearEdge3D Verity available in India?

Yes. WildPlantTS is an official ClearEdge3D master reseller in India, providing Verity software sales, demonstrations, training, implementation and local technical support.

What does ClearEdge3D Verity do?

ClearEdge3D Verity compares point cloud data from the as-built site against design and fabrication models to verify installation accuracy, identifying any deviations from the original plan.

Can Verity work with my existing LiDAR scanner?

Yes, Verity is completely hardware agnostic. It can process point cloud data generated from almost any terrestrial laser scanner, mobile mapper, or photogrammetry capture system.

Does Verity work with EPIC T150?

Absolutely. The EPIC T150 high-precision terrestrial laser scanner is perfectly suited for generating the dense, precise point clouds required for reliable Verity QA/QC analysis.

Does Verity integrate with Autodesk Revit?

Yes. Verity features native integration with Autodesk Revit, allowing you to push variance data and as-built locations seamlessly back into your native BIM environment.

Does Verity integrate with Autodesk Navisworks?

Yes. It serves as an add-in application for Autodesk Navisworks, allowing it to leverage Navisworks' powerful model coordination features for construction verification routines.

Can Verity be used for construction progress tracking?

Yes. By routinely comparing new scan data against the scheduled BIM model, project managers can objectively confirm what elements have been installed and track overall timeline progress.

What reports can Verity generate?

Verity allows you to generate comprehensive PDF and HTML reports featuring heat maps, color-coded item statuses, screenshots, and raw variance measurements to share with project stakeholders.

Can WildPlantTS demonstrate Verity using our project data?

Certainly. Through our Customer Dataset Trial, simply send us a registered point cloud and its corresponding BIM file, and our specialists will run a live evaluation tailored to your project.
