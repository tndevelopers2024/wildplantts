Mobile LiDAR Scanning for

Traditional surveying and reality capture workflows can involve multiple instrument setups, repeated measurements and significant field time.

Mobile LiDAR changes the way spatial data is captured.

A mobile LiDAR scanner combines laser scanning with technologies such as SLAM, cameras, IMU and positioning systems to continuously capture the surrounding environment as the operator moves through the project.

Instead of stopping at every measurement location, operators can walk, scan and map.

SLAM-Powered 3D Reality
Capture

Map complex environments without relying on continuous GNSS visibility.

SLAM — Simultaneous Localization and Mapping — enables a mobile LiDAR scanner to estimate its position while simultaneously building a 3D representation of the surrounding environment.

This makes SLAM LiDAR scanning particularly valuable in environments where conventional GNSS-based workflows become difficult.

PRECISE mobile LiDAR systems combine LiDAR measurements with positioning, imaging and inertial sensing to support continuous mapping as the operator moves through the environment.
